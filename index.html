Crowd Runner has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. It keeps your progress on your phone and never uploads it. Two kinds of data do leave the device, both handled by Google: information used to show ads, and gameplay statistics and crash reports that help us fix bugs and balance the game. This policy explains both.

1 Who is responsible

Crowd Runner is published by VOIDEN Apps. Questions about this policy, or about data handled through the game, can be sent to aiapps21@proton.me.

2 What the game stores on your device

Crowd Runner saves your progress locally, in a file inside the app's own private storage. That includes:

  • coin balance, purchased and selected skins, and upgrade levels;
  • achievements, best wave reached, best coins in a run, and total runs played;
  • daily-bonus streak and the date it was last claimed;
  • your sound setting.

This file stays on the device. It is not transmitted anywhere, it is not backed up to us, and we cannot read it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

3 Advertising — Google AdMob

Crowd Runner shows ads through Google AdMob, operated by Google. Two kinds appear: a video you can choose to watch to continue a run, and a full-screen ad that appears occasionally between runs.

To select, deliver, and measure those ads, Google collects and processes information from your device. That typically includes your advertising ID, your IP address (from which approximate location can be derived), and technical information about the device and the app, such as device model, operating-system version, and how you interacted with an ad.

We do not receive this information. We see only aggregate reporting — how many ads were shown and what they earned — which does not identify anyone.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's advertising practices: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4 Gameplay statistics and crash reports — Google Firebase

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, both operated by Google, to understand how the game is played and to find and fix crashes.

Gameplay statistics. The game reports a small set of in-game events: when a run starts and ends (the world played, the wave reached, how long the run lasted, coins earned, and whether a continue was used), when an ad is shown or tapped, and when something is bought in the in-game shop with in-game coins. Firebase also records some events on its own, such as the first launch of the app and the start of a session.

Crash reports. If the game crashes or hits an error, Crashlytics sends a report containing the technical details of the error, the state of the game at that moment (such as the current wave and world), and information about the device.

Along with these, Firebase collects technical identifiers and device information: an app-instance ID generated for this installation, the advertising ID where available, device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and approximate location (country or region) derived from the IP address.

None of this contains your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or anything you type. We see the results as statistics and crash lists in the Firebase console, and use them only to improve the game. Google keeps this data for a limited period — crash reports for about 90 days, analytics data for no more than 14 months.

How Google handles Firebase data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5 Who data is shared with

Data from the game is shared with these types of parties, and no others:

  • Advertising provider — Google (AdMob), to show and measure ads, as described in section 3.
  • Analytics and crash-reporting provider — Google (Firebase), to produce gameplay statistics and crash reports, as described in section 4.
  • Authorities — only if we are legally required to disclose information.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. Data is sent to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

6 What the game does not do

Crowd Runner does not ask you to create an account or sign in. It has no chat, no comments, no profiles, and no way for players to send anything to each other. It does not request your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files. It has no purchases for real money — the shop uses only coins earned in the game.

7 Permissions

The permissions the app declares come from the advertising and analytics libraries rather than from gameplay. The ones that matter for privacy are:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to fetch ads and send statistics and crash reports. Gameplay itself needs no network and works offline.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — access to the device advertising ID, used to select and measure ads and for analytics.

8 Your choices

Android gives you direct control over the advertising ID, independently of this app. In Settings → Privacy → Ads you can delete or reset it, and you can turn off ad personalisation entirely — ads will still appear, but they will be less targeted.

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored on your device and stops all data collection. A new installation gets a new app-instance ID, which is not linked to the old one.

Because we do not know who you are, we cannot look up data by your name or email. If you have a question or a request about your data, write to us and we will help as far as we can.

9 Children

Crowd Runner is not directed at children. It depicts stylised combat and is rated accordingly on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us and we will act on it.

10 Where you are

Google processes advertising and analytics data on servers in a number of countries, which may be outside the one you are in. Where local law requires your consent before personalised advertising is shown — such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland — that consent is requested inside the app before ads are loaded, and your choice is remembered and can be changed in the game's settings.
